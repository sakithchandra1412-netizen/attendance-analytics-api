## Examined and Decided NOT a Defect

1. **Storage of dates and shift times as plain strings:**
   `date` ("YYYY-MM-DD") and `shift_start` / `shift_end` ("HH:MM") are stored as strings in MongoDB. As detailed in `DATA_MODEL.md`, calendar dates and shift times are intentionally stored as strings rather than timestamps to prevent timezone pollution.
2. **Missing `half_day` or `history` fields on seeded/legacy documents:**
   `DATA_MODEL.md` notes that older records may omit `half_day` or `history`. Treating missing `history` as `[]` and missing `half_day` as `false` is the intended design for backwards compatibility.
3. **Module-level `MongoClient` instance:**
   PyMongo's `MongoClient` maintains its own internal connection pool and is thread-safe. Defining it once at module scope is standard practice in FastAPI.
